Items are the HMIS data-item numbers of the monthly state reports; "+" means the items are added. Each item is matched by its text in every financial year, so that item 4.1.2 (stillbirths before April 2017, preterm newborns from April 2017) is never read as the other. Deaths within 24 hours are reported by state only and are not modelled.

National ratio of the April 2017 rate to the March 2017 rate (descriptive; no ratio is beyond 15%): stillbirth rate 1.005, low birth weight share 1.014, weighing coverage 1.006. The 2017 format change enters Model T as a step; Model L has year-month fixed effects.

FY2020-21 was analysed as a sensitivity analysis and contributes no descriptive value here.
